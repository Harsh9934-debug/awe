This Candidates Privacy Notice (“Notice”) describes how Wonderful Systems Ltd. and its affiliates and subsidiaries (collectively, “Wonderful”, “we”, or “us”) collect and process Personal Data (as defined below) when you apply for a position with Wonderful (whether as an employee, worker, consultant, or independent contractor, and collectively, “Candidates”, “you”, or “your”), upon submission of your application, during the recruitment process, and, where applicable, thereafter.

This Notice applies to Candidates in the territories in which we offer job opportunities, including Israel, the European Economic Area, South Africa, and countries in Latin America, and is subject to all applicable data protection and privacy laws and regulations that govern, apply to, or may otherwise be relevant to our processing of Personal Data in the relevant jurisdiction(s) (collectively, “Data Protection Laws”), including, without limitation, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (“IPPL”) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the UK GDPR (together, the “GDPR”).

This Notice provides Candidates with information about the types of Personal Data we collect, how and why we use it, how long we retain it, with whom we share it, and your rights in relation to the Personal Data we process. This Notice also includes additional disclosures required under applicable Data Protection Laws, including (where the GDPR applies) our lawful bases for processing and other information we are required to provide to you under the GDPR.

This Notice supplements our Privacy Policy governing use of our website (if you submit your application through the website). If, following the recruitment process, you are engaged by Wonderful, your Personal Data collected during recruitment will be processed in accordance with our internal policies applicable to employees and other staff members, as updated from time to time.

3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) AND OTHER TOOLS

As part of our commitment to an efficient and fair recruitment process, we utilize artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities and automated tools to enhance our ability to evaluate candidates and conduct pre-employment verification. These technologies assist in managing the recruitment and hiring process, including candidate screening, assessment, and reviewing resumes, thus will process the Personal Data provided during the recruitment, however, and will not be used to substantially assist or replace discretionary employment decisions.

If you choose to have your first interview with Wonderful’s own developed AI agent first call asking about your professional experience, education, and skill sets will be conducted by the Agent and your Personal Data processed by it.

Please note that we do not make any automated decisions concerning your application without human involvement. Our AI tools are designed solely to support our talent acquisition team and do not make automated hiring decisions; all final decisions are made by our human interviewers and evaluators, based on a comprehensive consideration of various factors. Ensuring your data privacy and fair assessment are core priorities throughout this process, and we are dedicated to providing a balanced evaluation for every candidate.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your Personal Data in accordance with applicable laws and for as long as reasonably necessary to carry out the purposes described in this Notice, including to satisfy legal, regulatory, administrative, record keeping, and reporting requirements.

The criteria according to which we determine the retention periods are as follows:

  • Type of Personal Data and purpose of collection - We consider how long we need to retain the Personal Data to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, taking into account the scope, nature, and sensitivity of the Personal Data and the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure.

  • Stage of the recruitment process - We consider the stage of your application (for example, whether you were not selected or withdrew), as this may affect retention needs, including potential disputes or legal claims.

  • Legal obligations - Retention periods may depend on the laws of the applicable territory, including any minimum retention periods that apply. We may also retain Personal Data where required by a binding legal request, court order, or other valid legal process.

  • Disputes, claims, and legal proceedings - We may retain certain Personal Data where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Retention periods are typically determined by reference to applicable statutory limitation periods. If you exercise your rights in relation to your Personal Data, we may retain related records as necessary to demonstrate compliance, typically in accordance with such limitation periods.

  • Your reasonable expectations or consent - Depending on applicable law, we may retain Personal Data to consider you for future opportunities where this aligns with your reasonable expectations and/or where required, based on your consent. If you would like to opt out of retention for future opportunities, or withdraw consent where applicable, please contact us.

In addition to the above, we may retain limited Personal Data as a reference for any future applications submitted. If you are hired, we will store your Personal Data collected through the recruitment process for the term of your employment and thereafter, in accordance with our practices and policies related to our employees’ and staff members’ Personal Data.

Except as required by applicable law, we are not obligated to retain Personal Data for any specific period and may delete it in accordance with our retention practices. Where feasible, we will take reasonable steps to implement deletion in a timely manner.

9. PRIVACY RIGHTS

We acknowledge that different people have different privacy concerns and preferences. Our goal is to be clear about what Personal Data we collect so that you can make meaningful choices about how it is used. We allow you to exercise certain choices, rights, and controls in connection with your Personal Data.

Depending on your relationship with us, your jurisdiction and the applicable Data Protection Laws, these rights may include one or more of the following principal rights:

  • The right to know what Personal Data we collect about you, the purpose of collection, with whom we share your Personal Data, and additional information such as the categories of sources from which the Personal Data is collected – as provided under this Notice;

  • The right to access your Personal Data. This right entitles you to request access to, and receive a copy of, certain Personal Data we hold about you (subject to applicable law).

  • The right to correct your Personal Data. This right entitles you to request that incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated Personal Data we hold about you be corrected (and, where applicable, updated).

  • The right to request deletion of your Personal Data. This right entitles you to request us to delete Personal Data (subject to applicable Data Protection Laws, which permits or requires the retention of certain Personal Data);

  • The right to restrict processing of your Personal Data.This right entitles you to request that we limit the purposes for which your Personal Data is processed (subject to conditions under applicable Data Protection Laws).

  • The right to object to processing of your Personal Data.This right entitles you to object to certain processing, including where we process your Personal Data on the basis of legitimate interests (subject to conditions under applicable Data Protection Laws).

  • Data Portability - this right entitles you to receive the Personal Data you have provided, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller;

  • The right to withdraw consent where we process your Personal Data based on your consent.

  • The right to appeal or lodge a complaint. In the event that we do not fulfill your request, we will inform you without undue delay as required under applicable Data Protection Laws and you may have the right to appeal such decision. For EU/UK Candidates, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the applicable Data Protection Authority in the EU or the Information Commissioner in the UK.

For more information about your rights and how to exercise them, please refer to our Data Subject Request (“DSR”) form available on the Wonderful website and submit it to our Data Protection Officer at: dpo@wonderful.ai.

You may have additional rights as described in this Notice or applicable laws. We sometimes need to request specific information from you to help us confirm your identity and ensure the requested rights apply to you. This is another appropriate security measure to ensure that Personal Data is not disclosed to any person who has no right to receive it. Information provided in connection with such request will be processed only for the purpose of processing and responding to your request, and it may be shared with our legal and administrative teams.
